# v0.5.95

Telegram audio replies now wait until the complete recording is ready. The bot
keeps showing typing after the Codex turn finishes, prepares every audio part
in memory, sends the complete final text, then sends its voice notes. Typed
prompts keep their normal delivery behavior. If speech generation fails or
audio replies are disabled, the final text is released on its own.

Speech synthesis now preserves sentence boundaries and bounds each prompt using
the model's tokenizer. This works around an upstream sentence-splitting bug that passed oversized prompts
to Pocket TTS and could produce long, unintelligible audio. English and Italian
use the upstream recommended temperature, and speech preparation handles list
markers, version numbers, file paths, and digits in Italian identifiers. Empty or
unfinished generation is rejected rather than uploaded.

A database migration records preparation readiness without storing recordings.
Startup recovery clears readiness for audio lost from memory, while confirmed
text and audio delivery checkpoints are preserved. Disabling audio replies
interrupts synthesis, releases the shared CPU budget, and cancels pending audio.
Incoming recordings and generated audio continue to stay in memory only.
